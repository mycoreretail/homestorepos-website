Company

HomeStore POS is live

HomeStore POS is now running in furniture, mattress and home-goods stores across Georgia. It handles the whole store — quotes and sales, inventory and special orders, purchasing and receiving, delivery scheduling and routing, the warehouse floor, and the reporting an owner and their bookkeeper need at month end.

The software is cloud-based, so a store opens it on a register, a tablet or a delivery crew's phone with nothing to install and no server in the back office.

Integrations

Ashley Furniture Industries catalog integration

Dealers can connect the Ashley Furniture Industries catalog directly. Items come across with their vendor details, each store applies its own markup rules, and purchase orders go back to the vendor in Ashley's own order format.

Catalog updates arrive with cost changes flagged, so a dealer sees what moved before it reaches their price tags.

Product

Card terminals and payment processors connected by API

HomeStore POS connects to merchant service and payment processing companies by API, so the card terminal talks straight to the ticket rather than being keyed twice. At the end of the day, card batches reconcile against terminal totals inside the payments report.

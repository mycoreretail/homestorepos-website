What it does

The whole store, front to back.

No bolt-ons, no second system for the warehouse, no spreadsheet for the delivery board.

Sales, quotes & layaway

Quotes that convert to sales, layaway, deposits, protection plans, haul-away and setup, split commissions, package pricing and mixed tenders — all on one ticket.

Inventory, kits & catalog

Items, kits and packages with landed cost and automatic pricing, floor models kept separate from deliverable stock, and a picture-first catalog your team can sell straight from.

Customers & service

A full customer record — every quote, order, payment, delivery and note — with automatic follow-ups for check-ins, sleep trials, layaway payments, warranty claims and returns, and a record of where each customer came from.

Orders, purchasing & receiving

One board shows every open order and what it is waiting on. Buy what is not in stock, receive it against the customer who ordered it, and watch pickups and deliveries release themselves.

Delivery scheduling & routing

Book the stop at the register, schedule against real truck capacity, optimize the day, and send the crew out with the stop on their phone.

Reports & commissions

Sales analytics, top sellers, payments, commissions and a tax & accounting pack your bookkeeper can actually use — with exports when they want the file.

Label printing

Barcoded stock labels, price tags, hold tags and customer delivery labels — including box-of-box piece labels — straight to a Zebra printer or a letter sheet.

Time clock

Staff clock in and out from the register they already use, and hours and timesheets land in reports — no second device, no second app.

Goals, margins & limits

Margin targets, discount and margin limits per person, commission rules and scorecards — so everyone knows where they stand before month end, not after.

Lease-to-own & financing

Put several lease and finance companies on one ticket. Each line is tagged with who pays, the tax rule follows the company, and the invoice prints each company's portion.

Up system & close rate

Log every walk-in as it happens, see who took the up and what came of it, and get close rate and average ticket by salesperson — the numbers that tell you who is selling and who is just standing there.

Fraud & loss prevention

Big tickets attract bad actors. The system pushes back.

A furniture store takes four-figure card payments from people it has never met and loads merchandise onto trucks all day. HomeStore POS builds the checks into the workflow, so protecting the store does not depend on whether a new salesperson remembers.

Nothing here slows down an honest sale. It slows down the one that should have been stopped.

Card payments get verified
The register prompts the seller to confirm the card against the invoice and the customer's ID before the charge goes through, and records what was checked.
Same-day pickups get a warning
The pattern every furniture store gets hit with — a big card sale walked straight out the door — raises a caution on the screen where it can still be stopped.
Nothing leaves unpaid
Orders with a balance can't be picked up or loaded onto a truck. A manager can release one, and the release is recorded with their name.
Discounts need an approver
Each person has their own discount and margin limits. Going past them, voiding, or an out-of-policy return asks for a manager — and costs stay hidden from anyone not cleared to see them.
Proof the goods arrived
Signatures and photos at the stop, a signed slip at the counter, and a stamped record of who received what — the evidence you need when a chargeback shows up.
